      Fair play rules

      Snapshot, not average

      Rankings use the balance held at 00:00:00 ET. What you hold at midnight is what counts.

      One prize per wallet per day

      Each winning wallet receives one gift card code per daily round.

      Excluded wallets

      Liquidity pools, bridges, exchange wallets, the team wallet and burn addresses are never ranked.

      Claim window

      Winners have 7 days to reveal their code with the winning wallet. Unclaimed codes roll into future rounds.

      Anti-gaming

      Wallets splitting or cycling balances to manipulate rankings may be disqualified.

      Proof, not trust

      Balances, stakes, box openings and spins are all read from public blockchains. Anyone can verify them on the explorer.

      Questions from the clouds

      Do I need to stake to win the daily gift card?

      No. The Midnight Gift only needs $GIFT held in your wallet at 00:00 ET. Staking, Mystery Boxes and the Lucky Draw are optional islands. Note that tokens you stake leave your wallet while they are locked.

      Which chain is $GIFT on?

      Robinhood Chain.

      Where do the APR and rewards come from?

      Only from the staking contract: total staked, reward rate and your accrued rewards are read on-chain. If the contract doesn't expose enough data to compute APR, the island shows "—" instead of a guess.

      How do I receive my gift card?

      Connect the winning wallet and sign a free message (no gas, no transaction). Your code is revealed only to you.

      Can I choose the brand?

      Each day features one brand from the vault. Follow our X to see upcoming brands.

      Will anyone ever DM me for my seed phrase?

      Never. Pangea Gift will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Island transactions are always confirmed by you inside your own wallet.

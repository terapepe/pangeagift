      Robinhood Chain

      $GIFT lives on Robinhood Chain — one contract, one community, one leaderboard.

      Fair play rules

      Snapshot, not average

      Rankings use the balance held at 00:00:00 ET. What you hold at midnight is what counts.

      One prize per wallet per day

      Each winning wallet receives one gift card code per daily round.

      Excluded wallets

      Liquidity pools, bridges, exchange wallets, the team wallet and burn addresses are never ranked.

      Claim window

      Winners have 7 days to reveal their code with the winning wallet. Unclaimed codes roll into future rounds.

      Anti-gaming

      Wallets splitting or cycling balances to manipulate rankings may be disqualified.

      Proof, not trust

      Balances are read directly from public blockchains. Anyone can verify every winner on the Robinhood Chain explorer.

      FAQ

      Do I need to stake or register?

      No. Just hold $GIFT in a self-custody wallet. The snapshot finds you automatically.

      Which chain is $GIFT on?

      Robinhood Chain only. Use any EVM wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet…) to hold and check your $GIFT.

      When exactly is the snapshot?

      Every day at 00:00 New York time (ET), including weekends. The countdown on this page always shows the time left.

      How do I receive my gift card?

      Connect the winning wallet on this site and sign a free message (no gas, no transaction). Your code is revealed only to you.

      Can I choose the brand?

      Each day features one brand from the vault. Follow our X to see upcoming brands.

      Will anyone ever DM me for my seed phrase?

      Never. Pangea Gift will never ask for your seed phrase, private key or a token approval to claim a prize. Signing a plain message is all it takes.

      Midnight is coming.

      Make sure your wallet is on the board when the clock hits 00:00 ET.
